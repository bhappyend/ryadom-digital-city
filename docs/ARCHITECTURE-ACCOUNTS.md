## Monthly subscription clarification (human decision, 2026-11-27)

Paid business listings use a monthly subscription, not one-time placement. The legacy amountRub field means the same monthly RUB amount and must equal billing.monthlyPriceRub. Additive fields: PaidListingPricing.billing and ListingWithSubscription.subscription are optional for existing stage2 consumers; new producers must emit them. Absence means unknown/unavailable subscription state, never active/free. ListingSchema remains original shape. Listing responses use its additive projection. ListingSubscription statuses: pending has no funded period and no entitlement; active requires configured positive price, accepted immutable quote, independently confirmed real settlement and approved ownership/content, with startsAt and half-open currentPeriodStartsAt≤evaluatedAt<currentPeriodEndsAt. past-due means the funded period ended and the explicitly contracted grace window is still open; no grace agreement means immediate expired, never automatic invented credit. cancelled records cancellation now; cancelAtPeriodEnd=true preserves entitlement until funded end, then expired; immediate cancellation sets endedAt and ends entitlement immediately. expired has endedAt and no paid entitlement. Renewals extend only a newly settled period; missing/failed payment never extends it. Grace is contractual and bounded, represented graceEndsAt≥period end, not a fake payment. Serial transaction/CAS protects against duplicate settlement and simultaneous cancellation/renewal. No automatic charge, renewal request, provider credentials, subscription-success route or refund executor is implemented by this task.
